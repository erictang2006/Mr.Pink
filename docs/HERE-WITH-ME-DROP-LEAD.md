# Here With Me drop lead · Roses bars 37–44

The optional lead is a separate manually armed backing layer. Enable audio, select Roses, and use **Arm lead for bar 37** before that downbeat. Sandbox, Practice and fullscreen Performance show **HERE WITH ME LEAD — OFF / ARMED / PLAYING / DONE**, with independent overall volume, lower-layer volume and mute. The arm is never saved or enabled by song selection. No physical shortcuts, highlights, note trails or practice answers are generated by the lead.

For a short test in Sandbox or Practice, choose **Audition lead · bar 36** (one-bar lead-in) or **Audition lead · bar 37** (immediate entry). This explicit action clears the old transport take and practice loop, arms only the new lead, and starts at 100 BPM. Pause/Resume and Stop are beside the audition buttons. The existing vocal audition still requires starting before its original cue; a direct bar-37 audition does not start that vocal late.

**Start bar 25 + backing + lead** in Timed Practice explicitly arms the guitar/build bass, bar-29 buildup, and bar-37 lead together. It resets the old take and runs at 100 BPM; the vocal retains its selected entrance. Ordinary Start and song selection still do not arm the lead.
